About the Creators

William Hanna and Joseph Barbera were American animation directors, producers, and animators. They co-founded the animation studio Hanna-Barbera, which produced iconic shows like The Flintstones, The Jetsons, and of course, Tom and Jerry.

Did you know?

Tom and Jerry were created in 1940

Tom and Jerry were created by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera in 1940. The first episode, "Puss Gets the Boot," was released on February 10, 1940.

This iconic duo quickly became one of the most beloved and enduring animated pairings in history.

 Tom and Jerry have won numerous awards

Tom and Jerry have won numerous awards, including seven Academy Awards for Best Short Subject (Cartoon). They were also inducted into the Cartoon Hall of Fame in 1991.

Throughout their illustrious career, they have continued to captivate audiences worldwide with their timeless humor and slapstick antics.

 Their rivalry is legendary

The enduring rivalry between Tom and Jerry is one of the longest-running and most beloved in animation history.

Despite their constant chases and skirmishes, deep down, they share a unique bond that has resonated with generations of viewers.

Character Spotlight

Jerry

Jerry is a brown mouse who often outsmarts Tom.

  • First appearance: "Puss Gets the Boot" (1940)
  • Creator: William Hanna and Joseph Barbera

Tom

Tom is a grey and white domestic shorthair cat who often chases Jerry.

  • First appearance: "Puss Gets the Boot" (1940)
  • Creator: William Hanna and Joseph Barbera

Spike

Spike is a muscular bulldog who sometimes befriends Jerry and antagonizes Tom.

  • First appearance: "Dog Trouble" (1942)
  • Creator: William Hanna and Joseph Barbera

Butch

Butch is a black alley cat who often competes with Tom over Jerry or other pursuits.

  • First appearance: "Baby Puss" (1943)
  • Creator: William Hanna and Joseph Barbera

Tuffy

Tuffy, also known as Nibbles, is Jerry's young nephew who sometimes assists in his schemes.

  • First appearance: "The Milky Waif" (1946)
  • Creator: William Hanna and Joseph Barbera
